Privacy Notice

This page explains, in clear terms, how information may be handled when you visit this professional portfolio website or contact Sayed Asif.

1. Scope

This is a professional portfolio website for Sayed Asif. It provides general information about professional experience, software quality assurance skills, and publicly shareable work.

2. Information You Provide

This website provides an on-site contact form that allows visitors to send messages directly. The contact form is powered by Formspree, a third-party email service. You can also reach Sayed Asif by email at sayedasif83838@gmail.com, or through the WhatsApp button described in section 4.

Information you voluntarily submit through the contact form or by direct email may be received and used to respond to your enquiry. Form submissions are processed through Formspree and delivered directly to sayedasif83838@gmail.com. This website does not provide an account-registration system.

Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens, confidential source code, payment-card information, or other sensitive information through this website.

3. Technical Information

Website hosting infrastructure may process standard technical information required to deliver, maintain, and secure the site.

This can include information such as IP address, browser or device information, request timestamps, and server logs.

Such processing may be controlled by the hosting or infrastructure provider and may be subject to that provider's own privacy and security policies.

4. Third-Party Resources

The pages load their own code, styles, icons and fonts from sayedasif.com and do not load scripts, stylesheets or fonts from third-party servers. Earlier versions of the site loaded the Font Awesome icon stylesheet from the cdnjs content-delivery network; that was removed.

The Contact and Services forms send your message from your browser directly to Formspree, as described in section 2.

Every page has a WhatsApp button. Until you click it, it is a link to the Contact page. When you click it, your browser opens WhatsApp (wa.me, a service run by Meta) with a pre-filled message and Sayed Asif’s number, which the page assembles at that moment instead of showing it. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, this site does not record the click, and WhatsApp handles your message and technical data under its own privacy policy.

A browser request to a third-party resource may involve technical information being processed by that provider.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This portfolio does not operate an advertising tracker, an analytics service, or a user account system. The site’s own code does not set cookies or store data in your browser.

Google Analytics was previously loaded on two pages and has been removed from the site. Cookies it set earlier (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier) may remain in your browser until they expire or you delete them.

Third-party hosting, content-delivery, or infrastructure services may have their own technical mechanisms and policies.

6. How Information Is Used

Information voluntarily provided by email may be used to communicate with you, respond to professional enquiries, and handle legitimate correspondence.

Information is not intentionally collected through this portfolio for sale or advertising purposes.

7. Client, Employer and Project Confidentiality

This portfolio is not intended to disclose confidential client or employer information.

Project descriptions and examples are presented only at a general level where appropriate.

Nothing on this website should be interpreted as permission to access, reproduce, publish, or disclose proprietary systems, source code, credentials, personal data, internal documentation, or other confidential material belonging to an employer, client, or third party.

8. Security

Reasonable care is taken not to intentionally collect sensitive information through this public portfolio.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Notice

This notice may be updated when the website's functionality or information practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.
