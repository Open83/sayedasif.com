My QA Approach

Structured testing designed to identify defects early, provide useful evidence and reduce avoidable release risk.

01

Understand Requirements

Review requirements, user stories and business rules to understand expected behavior and identify areas that require additional validation.

02

Identify Risk Areas

Focus testing effort on workflows involving payments, permissions, calculations, integrations, data and other business-critical functionality.

03

Test Beyond the Happy Path

Validate boundary conditions, invalid inputs, failure scenarios, unexpected sequences and realistic user behavior.

04

Report Clearly

Provide reproducible defect reports with clear steps, expected results, actual results, evidence and useful severity information.
